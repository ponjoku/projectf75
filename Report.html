Changes in Nutritional Measurements

The figure shows mean MUAC and weight at baseline, stabilization, and discharge for children randomized to modified F75 or standard F75. Mean MUAC increased in both groups, while weight showed different patterns across measurements. The means are based on available measurements at each occasion, so the lines do not necessarily represent within-child changes.

OUTCOMES

Table 2 compares how children in each treatment arm did by the end of the study. Most children in both arms were discharged, 74% in the modified F75 arm and 80% in the discharged F75 arm. Deaths were more common in the modified F75 arm (17%) than i nthe standard F75 arm (12%). Withdrawals were similar between arms, among children who stabilized, time to stabilization was similar in both arms. Because these are interim data from the first 6 months of the trial, these patterns are descriptive and are not final results.
